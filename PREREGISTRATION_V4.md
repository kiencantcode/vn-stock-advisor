# Plan V4: every combination of model, entry and exit, judged on profit

Written 25 November 2026 before running. Already known: V3 results, and that on the one V3 model exits and dips did not help. 2023-26 is not a clean exam.

**Grid (16,800):** the 240 V3 models x 5 entries (next close; next open; limit at the signal close, else next close; wait for a 2% or 5% dip until the next review) x 14 exits (none; stop 7, 10, 15, 20%; take 10, 15, 20, 30%; trailing 10, 15, 20%; stop 10 + take 20; stop 15 + take 30). Exits trigger on a close and sell at the next close, not before 3 sessions after buying; the cash waits for the next review at 0%. 0.4% cost, ceiling/floor rules, averaged over start days.

**Eligible:** one-day top-N overlap of at least 85% (the owner needs steady lists). All 16,800 are also reported.

**Selection procedures,** each January from 2020, using only earlier returns, held for the year:
- A: highest yearly return. B: highest Sharpe. **C (primary): highest plateau return**, the mean yearly return of a setting and all settings differing in exactly one choice (score, smoothing, universe, step, N, entry, exit). C is less exposed to luck.

**Reported:** walk-forward return, worst fall, t against the E1VFVN30 fund and equal weight in 2020-26 and 2023-26; PBO; reality check over the whole grid.

**What goes live:** procedure C's pick on all data to 23 November 2026, if C's walk-forward return in 2023-26 is above the V3 model's 14% a year. Otherwise V3 stays. It stays paper only unless walk-forward 2023-26 beats both benchmarks with t >= 1.65.
